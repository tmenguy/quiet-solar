### [should-fix] Item 2: a reset switches the phases of a car that stays stopped
- File: `custom_components/quiet_solar/ha_model/charger.py:2094` (source), `:2471-2480` (fix site)
- Severity: should-fix
- Source: qs-review-edge-case-hunter
- Description: on a reset, `_do_prepare_budgets_for_algo` sets
  `budgeted_num_phases = min(possible_num_phases)` for every charger, and a car
  that ends at 0 A keeps that value. `apply_budgets` then applies it, so a stopped
  car on a switchable wallbox is asked to go from 3 phases to 1 while staying off.
  `possible_num_phases` stays `[1, 3]` even for a locked car (`possible_amps == [0]`),
  because the phase choice at `:3175-3178` does not look at the lockout. The path
  predates the PR, but RC-D makes it frequent, because stopped chargers now stay
  listed on every cycle. Expected effect, from reading the code and not run on a
  real charger: `_ensure_correct_state` drives the switch and blocks the group, then
  `TIME_OK_BETWEEN_CHANGING_CHARGER_PHASES` (30 min) keeps the bump car on one phase.
- Proposed fix: in `apply_budgets`, skip the phase request when `new_state` is False
  and `init_state` is not True (a car that stays stopped). Do not fix this at `:2094`
  on `possible_amps == [0]`: a free stopped car that ends at 0 A has the same flip.
  Keep the QS-381 comment and its live-count comparison: a budget above 0 A, and a
  running car being stopped, must still apply their phase count.
- Tests (red first, per the lane's red-test protocol):
  - A stopped car with `[1, 3]`, both locked (`[0]`) and free (`[0, 6..16]`), ends a
    reset pass at 0 A: expected phases stay 3 and the charge state stays False.
  - Guard: a reset that gives a stopped car more than 0 A at 1 phase still applies it.
  - Guard: a running car being stopped keeps today's phase behaviour.
- Docs: add the rule to `docs/agents/concepts/charger-budgeting.md` and update
  `last_verified`. Add this file to the story's progress note as a reasoned amendment
  of the fix plan.

### [residual] Item 1: record R7, with no code change
- File: `docs/stories/QS-448.story.md` (blast radius and "Residuals after the fix"),
  and "Known limits" in the PR #450 body
- Severity: should-fix (the user chose to accept it as a residual)
- Source: qs-review-edge-case-hunter
- Description: `has_green_charger` (`charger.py:1620`) is true as soon as a green car is
  listed. Since RC-D, a stopped green car stays listed on every cycle, so a group whose
  only charging car is forced (`auto_price` or `auto_consign`) now has the phantom-surplus
  subtraction and the production cap on every cycle, not about one in seven. These can
  lower a forced car's amps above its floor. They cannot stop it: a forced command never
  offers 0 A.
- **Do not change `has_green_charger`.** The two options reviewed were rejected:
  - `possible_amps != [0]` only covers the 10-min lockout, not a free stopped car.
  - Ignoring stopped green cars removes the guards at the moment they would prevent a
    wrong start, and the car then cannot stop for 20 min (ON→OFF lockout).
- Proposed fix: add R7 to the story with this evidence, from the production log of
  2026-10-29 to 2026-11-02, written before this fix. Cycles where a forced car charges and
  no green car charges:

  | | stopped green car listed | no green car listed |
  |---|---|---|
  | cycles | 46 | 4 088 |
  | surplus available (`full_available_home_power >= 0`) | 1 | 5 |
  | phantom surplus subtracted | 10 | 0 |
  | production cap or cap reset | 0 | 0 |
  | forced car reductions | 1 | 27 |

  The one reduction with a listed green car was 10→9 A at about −5.8 kW with no green rule
  triggered in that cycle. A forced car almost always imports from the grid, so its budget
  is already negative with or without these rules. Limits to state with R7: this fix makes
  these cycles much more frequent, 46 cycles is a small sample, and these few days do not
  cover a summer day with surplus during a forced charge. Reopen with a real log if that
  case shows up.
