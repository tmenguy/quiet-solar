### [should-fix] R4-S1 — the `control-plane.md` mirror applies "head moved" to a pending review
- File: `docs/workflow/control-plane.md:676`
- Severity: should-fix
- Source: qs-review-blind-hunter and qs-review-edge-case-hunter, merged
- Description: the doc says for both `cr: trigger` and `cr: pending` that "any other error, or a head that moved,
  skips it at once". The template skips only a `trigger` on a moved head (qs-node.md.j2 ~:324-325). A `pending`

### [should-fix] R4-S2 — plan-concrete cannot run the create-target-at-base check (R3-S2 is not actually fixed)
- File: `scripts/qs/agent_templates/qs-node-plan-concrete.md.j2:40` and `:65-68`

- Source: qs-review-blind-hunter and qs-review-edge-case-hunter, merged

  round of this task may have created it)." Keep the wrong-parent-directory or wrong-module-path `should_fix`
  rule. Pin the new sentence in `test_plan_concrete_file_specificity_agrees_with_create_targets`, and assert the
  `git merge-base` wording is gone from this template. Update the story's implementation note.
