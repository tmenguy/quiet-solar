## Findings to fix

### [should-fix] I1 — Restore: disk-full while keeping the live DB is still reported as "move it aside" (H1 gap)
- File: `restore.py:271-278` (the `backups.write_copy` call for the
  `replaced` copy) and `restore.py:315-321` (outer `except sqlite3.DatabaseError`)
- Severity: should-fix
- Source: qs-review-edge-case-hunter (should-fix), qs-review-blind-hunter (nice-to-have)
- Description:
  - SQLite reports a full disk during `src_conn.backup(copy)` (in
    `backups.write_copy`) as `sqlite3.OperationalError("database or disk is
    full")`, not as `OSError`.
  - The H1 guard catches only `OSError`, so this error falls through to the
    outer `CONFLICT` "the live DB cannot be kept or restored — move it
    aside".
  - That is the misleading hint H1 was meant to remove. The live DB is
    fine; the backup directory is full.
- Proposed fix:
  - Around that `write_copy` call, catch `(OSError, sqlite3.OperationalError)`
    and send it to the existing H1 refusal: `CONFLICT`, "cannot be kept …
    free space in (or fix) {dest.parent}", with no "move it aside".
  - Corruption of the live DB itself (`SQLITE_CORRUPT` / `NOTADB`, i.e.
    `sqlite3.DatabaseError` that is not `OperationalError`) stays on the
    existing "move it aside" path.
  - Test: make the copy's `backup` raise `OperationalError("database or
    disk is full")`. Expect `CONFLICT`, a hint naming free space and not
    "move it aside", and the live DB unchanged.
