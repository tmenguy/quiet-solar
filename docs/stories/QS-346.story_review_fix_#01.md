## Findings to fix

### [must-fix] F1 — Cross-charger car attribution in `_notify_charger_fault`
- File: `custom_components/quiet_solar/ha_model/charger.py:4801`
- Severity: must-fix
- Source: qs-review-edge-case-hunter + qs-review-coderabbit (2 reviewers)
- Description: `car = self.car or self._last_attached_car` selects the
  remembered car **without** the `self._last_attached_car.charger is None`
  discriminator that `_check_plugged_val` (charger.py:4483-4484) applies.
  When charger A faults after its car (Zoe) was detached and re-homed to
  charger B, A's 120 s debounce broadcast tells the whole household to
  "unplug and replug Zoe on A" — but Zoe is charging on B. Wrong car
  attribution in a critical household-wide alert.
- Proposed fix: compute a single trusted car once, reusing the guarded
  expression:
  `car = self.car or (self._last_attached_car if self._last_attached_car is not None and self._last_attached_car.charger is None else None)`
  Use that `car` for both the message text and (see F3) recipient routing.

### [must-fix] F2 — AC-1 observable behavior not tested
- File: `tests/test_charger_additional_coverage.py` (new test)
- Severity: must-fix
- Source: qs-review-acceptance-auditor
- Description: AC-1 promises (a) the faulted charger re-attaches a car
  within one cycle and (b) `get_charge_type()` returns
  `CAR_CHARGE_TYPE_FAULTED`. The only AC-1 test
  (`test_check_plugged_val_trusts_remembered_car_ac1`) asserts just the
  enabling precondition `is_plugged(...) is True`. Neither the actual
  re-attach nor the `CAR_CHARGE_TYPE_FAULTED` result — the whole point of
  the story (restore the red car card) — is verified.
- Proposed fix: add a cycle-level test that seeds the post-detach state
  (`_last_attached_car = zoe`, `zoe.charger = None`, plug probe PLUGGED,
  unfiltered status `Faulted`), runs one
  `check_load_activity_and_constraints`, then asserts a car is attached
  (`charger.car is not None`) and `get_charge_type()[0] == CAR_CHARGE_TYPE_FAULTED`.
  The story implementer notes (story lines ~402-410) describe this seeding.

### [should-fix] F3 — Notification channels not failure-isolated; remembered person unreachable
- File: `custom_components/quiet_solar/ha_model/charger.py:4810-4811`
- Severity: should-fix
- Source: qs-review-coderabbit
- Description: (a) If `on_device_state_change` raises, the household
  `async_notify_all_mobile_apps` broadcast never runs, the exception
  propagates out of `_notify_charger_fault`, and the caller (line 3603)
  never sets `_charger_fault_notified` — so every later cycle retries the
  same fault episode. (b) `on_device_state_change` selects recipients from
  `self.car`; when `self.car is None` but a trusted remembered car exists,
  its person cannot be notified.
- Proposed fix: wrap each of the two notification channels in its own
  `try/except` (log-and-continue) so one channel failing cannot suppress
  the other, and let `_notify_charger_fault` return normally so the
  episode latches after both attempts. Pass the F1 trusted car into
  recipient selection so the remembered car's person is reachable when
  `self.car is None`. Add coverage for a re-homed remembered car (no
  wrong-car message) and for a detached remembered car with a forecasted
  person.

### [should-fix] F4 — AC-4 constraint retention not exercised across a real cycle
- File: `tests/test_charger_rebalancing_scenarios.py`
- Severity: should-fix
- Source: qs-review-acceptance-auditor
- Description: `test_is_load_active_false_while_faulted_reverts_ac4`
  inspects `charger.car`/`charger._constraints` statically but never runs
  a "full faulted cycle" (`check_load_activity_and_constraints`). Constraint
  survival across a real cycle is asserted statically, not exercised.
- Proposed fix: extend/add a test that runs one full
  `check_load_activity_and_constraints` on a faulted charger with a car +
  constraint attached, then asserts both `charger.car` and the constraint
  are still present afterward, and that they revert (charger re-enters
  `active_loads`) once the fault clears.
