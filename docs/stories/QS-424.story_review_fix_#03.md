## Findings to fix

### [must-fix] H1 — the export's prefix regexes backtrack exponentially
- File: `scripts/qs/control_plane/export.py` (`_PREFIX`, `_SETEXT_RE`)
- Source: edge-case-hunter
- Description: `(?:[ \t]*>[ \t]?|…)*` splits each space two ways: a line `"> " * 40 + "x"` (82 bytes) never returns; `export-summary` hangs.
- Proposed fix: possessive repetitions (`*+`), output unchanged (the reviewer's 200,000-value differential fuzz); a bounded-time test on 5000-level lines.

### [must-fix] H2 — the wrapped-nonce scrub runs after `redact`, so most of the nonce leaks
- File: `scripts/qs/control_plane/tools.py` (`_launch`)
- Source: edge-case-hunter
- Description: `redact` eats the hex before the wrap, and the whole-nonce pattern then cannot match the rest (22 of 32 characters recorded).
- Proposed fix: scrub the nonce first, then `redact`; the wrapped test asserts no 8-character slice of the nonce.

### [should-fix] H3 — the fence tracker falls out of step with CommonMark
- File: `scripts/qs/control_plane/export.py` (`_value_lines`)
- Source: blind-hunter, acceptance-auditor, edge-case-hunter
- Description: a fence behind a list or quote marker (`- ```` `, `> ```` `), a marker-prefixed or 4-space "closer", a tab-indented opener: a later heading stays un-demoted (a quoted h1 under `## Digest`).
- Proposed fix: track only a bare fence (no marker, at most three columns with tabs expanded), closed by a bare closer, or — indented — by a less-indented line; everything else errs on over-demotion. Tests for each repro.

### [should-fix] H4 — deeply nested JSON is INTERNAL, not USAGE

- Source: edge-case-hunter
