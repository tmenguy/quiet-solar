## Findings to fix

### [should-fix] EC2-1 — the reservation can push a healthy forced member down to 0 A
- File: `custom_components/quiet_solar/ha_model/charger.py` (`_do_prepare_and_shave_budgets`, `_shave_mandatory_budgets`)
- Source: qs-review-edge-case-hunter (reproduced against the real shave)
- Description: when the reservation fits under the group max but reservation + the members' floor
  amps does not, `_shave_mandatory_budgets` lowers a healthy member's minimum down to 0. The shave
  then "succeeds", so the #01 retry never runs, and the healthy charger is stopped to keep amps for
  a charger that draws nothing. This is a milder form of the QS-376 starvation.
- Proposed fix: in `_do_prepare_and_shave_budgets`, right after `_do_prepare_budgets_for_algo` and
  before any shave: if a reservation exists and the members' floor amps
  (`mandatory_amps`) plus the reservation are not acceptable, release the reservation for this
  cycle. The reservation only limits the growth of the other members; it never forces them below
  their minimums. This replaces the #01 retry in `budgeting_algorithm_minimize_diffs`.
  Test it against the real `_do_prepare_and_shave_budgets` with a limit-checking
  `is_current_acceptable`.

### [should-fix] EC2-2 — the #01 retry reuses minimums already lowered under the reservation
- Same file / fix as EC2-1: the release now happens before any `possible_amps` mutation, and the
  post-failure retry is removed. The test covers a reservation larger than the group max: after
  the release the forced member keeps 6 A.

### [nice-to-have] EC2-3 — release log not rate-limited
