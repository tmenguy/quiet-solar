# QS-448 — Bug: ID Buzz charges instead of low-SOC solar-priority Tesla

- Issue: https://github.com/tmenguy/quiet-solar/issues/448
- Lane: bug × product · Branch: `QS_448`
- Status: diagnosis v6 (review rounds 1–3 folded in, start block added, R1–R6 accepted, decrease rule rejected)

## Symptom

On 2026-11-02 (sunny day), the Tesla M3 sat at about 7 % SOC on `wallbox 3 portail`
with **solar priority** (`qs_bump_solar_charge_priority`) ON. No person was
assigned to it, so it had only a `filler` constraint. Meanwhile the ID.buzz,
already at about 37 % SOC on `wallbox 2 parking` with no bump, got the solar
surplus. This happened twice in the morning, and the Tesla stayed idle for
about 23 min (10:13→10:37) and about 22 min (11:15→11:37).

The user's expectation: whenever the Tesla can charge, the solar surplus goes
to it, and a lower-priority, after-battery car must not use the battery's share
of the surplus. This fix delivers that subject to the accepted residuals R1–R6.
The acceptance criteria are in "Acceptance".

## Root cause

Three defects in `charger.py` together turn "the bump car is stopped for
one cycle by a production dip" into "the lower-priority car takes the
surplus for ~20 min":

**RC-B (trigger: the intruder starts).** The before/after-battery budget in
`QSChargerGroup.budgeting_algorithm_minimize_diffs`
([charger.py:1592-1601](custom_components/quiet_solar/ha_model/charger.py:1592))
is computed once for the whole group. If any charger in the list is before
battery, `initial_power_budget = full_available_home_power`, battery share
included, and that budget is shared by every charger in the increase loop
([charger.py:1790-1850](custom_components/quiet_solar/ha_model/charger.py:1790)).
So an after-battery charger (ID.buzz) can start or grow on power the solver
reserved for the battery, just because a stopped before-battery charger
(Tesla) is listed.

RC-B has a second part. Nothing stops a lower-priority after-battery car from
starting while the bump car waits out its OFF→ON lockout, even on surplus
beyond the battery's ask. At 10:19:00 the log shows 1339 W with the ID.buzz
drawing about 4250 W, so about 5590 W without it: 5590 − 4254 − 1028 = +308 W.
So the ID.buzz could start about 3 min after the blocked 10:15:58 start, and
then hold its own lockout against the Tesla. The Zoe would be reached first
(5590 − 4480 − 1028 = +85), because it sorts above the ID.buzz. The start block
stops both.

The gate only protects the ask as currently computed, and that depends on the
solver's plan. The ask is 5522 W at 10:15:09 and 1028 W at 10:15:58, and the
Tesla holds a consign of about 4290 W by then. No log line states the link, so
this is an inference: the ask seems to have fallen because the solver planned
that power for the Tesla. On that reading, only about 1 kW of the ID.buzz's
4254 W at 10:15:58 was really the battery's ask, and most of the rest was power
planned for the Tesla. That is why the gate alone cannot fix the first incident.
In the second, the consign is 0 and the ask stays high, so the gate is enough.

**RC-A (the intruder outranks the bump car).**
`QSChargerGeneric.get_stable_dynamic_charge_status`
([charger.py:3195-3196](custom_components/quiet_solar/ha_model/charger.py:3195))
adds the "forced to charge" boost `native_score_span` (1 000 000) to any
charger whose `possible_amps[0] > 0`. That includes a plain green charger
that only cannot stop because of its 20-min ON→OFF hysteresis
([charger.py:3091-3093](custom_components/quiet_solar/ha_model/charger.py:3091)
and [3131-3150](custom_components/quiet_solar/ha_model/charger.py:3131)).
So the ID.buzz (≈ 1 000 4xx) outranks the bump Tesla (≈ 72 400 =
`(24 before-battery + 48 bump)·1000 + battery term`). As a result:

- it is `actionable_chargers[0]` (the "best charger") in
  `budgeting_algorithm_minimize_diffs`
  ([charger.py:1461-1506](custom_components/quiet_solar/ha_model/charger.py:1461)),
  so the "stop others so the best one can charge" reset never runs, and
  neither does its `cs_to_stop_by_forcing_it` path built for this exact
  lockout;
- it ranks ahead of the Tesla on every increase pass. Removing the boost does
  not by itself put a stopped Tesla first: see R4.

**RC-D (the bump car disappears from budgeting).** In
`QSChargerGroup.apply_budgets`
([charger.py:2386-2423](custom_components/quiet_solar/ha_model/charger.py:2386)),
a stopped charger is re-applied every cycle with `new_amp =
charger_default_idle_charge` (6) against `init_amp = 0`.
`current_real_max_charging_amp` is forced to 0 when the state is off
([charger.py:3042-3043](custom_components/quiet_solar/ha_model/charger.py:3042)).
So `_last_amp_change_time = time` is re-armed on every apply, even though
nothing physical changed. The "Fix D" amp-change cooldown in
`QSChargerGroup.dyn_handle`
([charger.py:1315-1331](custom_components/quiet_solar/ha_model/charger.py:1315))
then drops the stopped charger from `budget_chargers` for 45 s
(`CHARGER_ADAPTATION_WINDOW_S`). Budgeting runs about every 7 s while another
charger keeps it going, so the stopped Tesla is excluded **permanently**:

- no restart, even with about 4 kW of surplus (11:28);
- no reset, because it is not in the list;
- no priority check.

Fix D's purpose was to let a charger's 45-s median power window refresh
after a **real** current change, to stop double-stepping over the inverter
limit (#66, `af775016`). A charger that stays stopped draws nothing and has
nothing to settle.

The Tesla's occasional full-list cycles (10:24:45, 10:25:34, 10:26:23) fit
this. A real change on the ID.buzz holds the whole group for more than 45 s
through `verified_correct_state_time`, so the Tesla's cooldown lapses. The
gaps 10:29:19→10:37:01 and 11:17:11→11:34:49 have no real change, so the
Tesla stays hidden.

Causal chain:

1. A short production dip makes the decrease pass stop the bump car. This is
   normal green-only behaviour.
2. The bump car is OFF→ON locked for 10 min (`possible_amps [0]`).
3. **RC-B:** being before battery, it opens the full surplus to the group,
   and the after-battery ID.buzz starts on a budget that includes the battery's
   ask (10:15:58, 11:16:15). Even in its locked state, the bump car being listed
   is enough to trigger this.
4. **RC-A:** the ID.buzz is now ON→OFF locked for 20 min, so it gets
   +1 000 000 and ranks first. There is no reset, and it takes the increments
   on full-list cycles (up to 9 A).
5. **RC-D:** once the bump car's own lockout ends, it is still hidden by
   the re-armed cooldown on most cycles. It cannot restart even with ample
   surplus, nor trigger a reset.
6. The situation only ends incidentally. At 10:36:05 the ID.buzz leaves its
   lockout and a battery-reservation decrease (Tesla not listed, so the
   budget is all-after) stops it. At 11:37:02 a cycle where the cooldown
   lapsed lists the Tesla, which now outranks the unboosted ID.buzz, and
   the reset fires.

Hypotheses eliminated:

- *ID.buzz had the bump.* Only the Tesla had it, from 09:43:57 to the
  10:28:56 toggles and again from 10:29:00.
- *Car↔charger mis-assignment / "no driver attached".* The Tesla stayed on
  `wallbox 3 portail` (user pin). Having no person only means filler-only
  constraints, so nothing mandatory outranked the bump. That is context,
  not the cause.

**Demonstrated (RC-A)** with a throwaway uncommitted pytest:
`assert 48400 > 1000400` fails. That fixture is after-battery, hence 48 400;
production is ≈ 72 400. RC-B and RC-D are shown by the log lines quoted
above and by reading the code. Their red tests are specified below.

Throwaway runs on today's code also confirmed four red specs below: test 5
(`cs_buzz` 6 A at 4289 W), test 5b (7 A at 1000 W), test 11 (Tesla → 6 A, ID.buzz
stays 9 A, with computed scores 72 370 and 1 000 370), and test 12 (`cs_buzz`
6 A at 5590 W, with ask 1028 and with ask 0). Guards 10 and 13 behaved as
specified on the same code. Those runs do not prove the tests go green, because
the fix was not applied. Tests 1–4 and 6 were only traced by hand.

### Blast radius

**RC-A** changes `charge_score` only for green-family chargers
(`AUTO_GREEN_ONLY` / `GREEN_CAP`, and `GREEN_CONSIGN` without battery
discharge) during their ≤ 20-min ON→OFF lockout. They rank by real priority
instead of +1 000 000. Every `charge_score` consumer in `charger.py`:

- 1028 (`ensure_correct_state`) sets only the initial order; every later
  consumer re-sorts.
- 1461 (reset "best" pick) is the intended change, with a wider reach than the
  bump car. The 1475 branch has no bump condition and today a boosted locked
  car hides it. So any stopped, free, higher-scoring car can now force-stop a
  green car once that car has been on for 10 min, and the 1485 branch fires
  10 min earlier for a charging bump car. This shortens the 20-min ON→OFF hold,
  within the reset limit.
- 1765 (increase/decrease ordering) is now priority-consistent.
- 1939 (auto-price extension) skips only `GREEN_ONLY` and `GREEN_CAP`
  (`charger.py:1942`), so it can reach a `GREEN_CONSIGN` charger when a price
  charger triggers the path. The effect is a tie-break there, which is small.
- 2080 (`_shave_current_budgets`) respects `possible_amps`, so a locked
  charger is still not stopped there.
- **2153 (`_shave_mandatory_budgets`)** is a behaviour change. On a group
  over-current, this shave can go **below** `possible_amps[0]` and stop a
  locked charger. A locked lower-priority charger used to sort last and is
  now shaved in priority order, i.e. **before** higher-priority chargers.
  That is the intended priority semantics. Only the order changes: the
  `possible_amps[0] > 0` check at line 2174 is not edited.

**RC-D:** stopped chargers (and chargers being stopped) no longer arm the
45-s cooldown. So they stay visible to budgeting, the reset check and
ordering. Starts and amp changes on a charging charger are unchanged.
`dyn_handle` is the only production reader of `_last_amp_change_time`.
Consequences:

- **Cadence:** with nothing charging, budgeting runs about every 7 s instead of
  about 49 s, because the all-in-cooldown skip no longer applies. `dyn_handle`
  runs once per charger callback (`charger.py:6637`), so with 3 chargers this
  goes from 1 budgeting list per 49 s to about 21, plus the forbid lines for each
  listed car. That is about 21 times more log lines and start decisions.
- **Reset timer:** cycles that hid the stopped best charger used to clear
  `_last_time_should_reset_budget_received`. It now survives, so the 10-min
  override (`charger.py:1308-1313`) becomes reachable. Effective reset rate can
  rise from one per 20 min towards one per 10 min. The constants are unchanged.
- **No new overshoot or double-decrease:** a real change clears the verified
  state, which holds the group for more than 45 s (`charger.py:1078-1081`).
  This is what actually protects against double-stepping. The log gaps after
  real changes fit it: 10:13:24→10:14:20, 10:19:14→10:20:03, 10:23:56→10:24:45.

**RC-B:** after-battery chargers in groups that also contain a
before-battery charger can no longer start or grow into the battery's share
while `battery_asked_charge > 0`. These cases are unchanged:

- groups that are all before battery or all after battery;
- `battery_asked_charge <= 0`;
- decreases on a normal pass;
- homes without a battery (`battery_asked_charge = 0`).

Other reach of the gate:

- **Reset passes:** a reset drops chargers to their floor and rebuilds them
  through the increase branch. So on a reset the gate **does** shrink a running
  after-battery car with a 0 floor to the surplus beyond the ask. This covers
  the 1485 bump reset, the 1552 current path and the 1708 production-cap path.
  It fits the battery-reservation intent.
- **Auto-price path (`charger.py:1908-1990`)** is not gated, and neither is the
  start block applied there. It skips only `GREEN_ONLY` and `GREEN_CAP`, and the
  chargers it reaches count as before battery when a constraint exists. The
  exception is a charger with no constraint (`charger.py:3202`), which is flagged
  after battery and could still be extended there.
- **Non-green chargers:** the predicate is not limited to green commands. An
  after-battery charger on a forced command is gated above its minimum in a
  mixed group.
- **Higher-priority after-battery cars:** a mandatory car on `GREEN_CAP` counts
  as after battery, so the gate applies to it while a lower-scoring
  before-battery car takes the full budget. This is the same fact used to
  reject the decrease rule. The gate only reserves the battery's share, so it
  never stops that car.
- **Phase switching:** a switchable wallbox rejected at 3 phases can still start
  at 1 phase on the phase-change pass, if the lower power fits the gate. On
  2026-11-02 neither incident car changed phase (always 3).
- **Ungated by design:** `_do_prepare_budgets_for_algo` and
  `_shave_current_budgets` only prepare or reduce.

**Start block:** applies only while a bump car is stopped and locked off, to
after-battery cars that are really not charging and score lower. It never stops
a car. Unlike the gate, it has no battery or group condition: it also applies
with an ask of 0 or below, without a battery, and in an all-after group. Cost:
for up to 10 min per stop, surplus the ID.buzz could have used goes to the
battery or is exported. A full, unplugged, faulted or car-side-stopped car is not
listed, so it blocks nothing. A misconfiguration with `min_charge > max_charge`
also gives `[0]` and would block permanently, but a normal setup does not reach
it. See R5 and R6.

Untouched: hysteresis constants, `possible_amps` construction, the
`_shave_mandatory_budgets` `possible_amps[0] > 0` test (line 2174, physical
meaning), Fix D's 45-s window, and the reset constants
(`TIME_OK_BETWEEN_BUDGET_RESET_S`).

The start block makes R2, R3 and R4 rarer for the bump car, because an
after-battery car does not start during the bump car's lockout. They still
apply when that car was already running.

- **R1:** a bump car is still stopped by a production dip (green only, no grid
  import). The dip can be short, because available power is the lowest of four
  averages and medians over 45 s. The car then waits its own 10-min OFF→ON
  lockout, and restarting needs about 4.3 kW at 3 phases. The user accepted
  this on 2026-11-05: the lockout is deliberate, to avoid fast on/off cycling.
  No follow-up issue.
- **R2:** in a mixed group, on a normal pass, an after-battery charger that is
  **already running** is not shrunk to give the battery its share. Only its
  start/growth is gated (see fix step 3), and a reset does shrink it. RC-D keeps
  the Tesla listed, so **every** all-after battery-reservation decrease stops
  happening while the Tesla is listed, not just the 10:36:05 stop. For example,
  the ID.buzz would hold 9 A against a 5.1 kW ask at 10:26:24, until a reset.
  - With a bump car and a car already running when it stopped, the bound is about
    10 min after the stop (the end of the lockout, or the other car's start + 10
    min if later), plus any reset throttling.
  - With a charging **non-bump** before-battery car, neither reset branch fires
    and the budget stays positive, so there is **no time limit**. This predates
    the fix.
  - The user accepted this on 2026-11-05, with these corrected figures.
- **R3:** a before-battery car at its minimum can still be stopped by a dip

  car's first 10 min on, or while resets are throttled. A before-battery car

  (`charger.py:1485` requires the bump), so the exposure lasts up to the other
  car's full 20-min lockout. Once stopped and past its own 10-min lockout, it
  can recover through the 1475 branch, which has no bump condition. The user
  accepted this on 2026-11-05 as the cost of the anti on/off design.
- **R4:** a stopped, free Tesla can still lose to a running after-battery car

- **R5:** the start block only applies to after-battery cars. A lower-scoring,

- **R6:** a bump car whose start keeps failing repeats the block. Its retries run

  one.

  averages and medians over 45 s (`_extracts_power_value_from_data`). With the
  Tesla at 8 A and the ID.buzz at 6 A, a 1 kW dip would cut 4.2 kW and hold the

  at 7 A or below has no such margin: at 10:13:24 the same size of dip stopped
  it, because 1 A only frees about 690 W.

  (`charger.py:1302-1313`).
- It adds little. The 10-min gate is the reset's own (`charger.py:1514`), and
  the reset already runs first for a charging bump car (`charger.py:1485`). A
  reset at T stops the car, which can restart at T+10 and reach 10 min on at
  T+20, exactly when the next reset is allowed. The first 10 min, the real
  remaining gap, stay uncovered.
- It is awkward to place: the budget's sign is known only after prepare and
  shave, and a 3→1 phase switch can run before any stop is allowed
